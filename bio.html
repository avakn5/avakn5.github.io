I am an AI researcher interested in reinforcement learning, world models, AI safety, computer systems, and medical imaging.

About me

I completed my master's in Computational and Mathematical Engineering at Stanford in June 2026. At Stanford, I researched reinforcement learning and world models in Professor Gordon Wetzstein's lab.

I also developed a deeper systems background through courses in operating systems and parallel computing, and served as a graduate teaching assistant for CS229: Machine Learning.

Before my master's, I worked with Dr. Mengyu Wang at Harvard on image segmentation and vision-language models for fairness in medical imaging. I later worked with Dr. Craig Levin at Stanford, applying latent diffusion models to PET image reconstruction.

My work includes CoverAgent, a behavioral evaluation of whether AI assistants preserve accurate responsibility attribution when another agent pressures them to conceal fault. I am currently looking for my next opportunity in industry.
